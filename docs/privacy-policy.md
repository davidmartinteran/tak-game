# Privacy Policy for TAK Game

**Last Updated:** October 29, 2025

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how TAK Game ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information Stored Locally

TAK Game stores the following information locally on your device:

- **Game State:** Your current game progress, including board position, player turns, and move history
- **Game Settings:** Your preferences for board size, sound settings, and other game configurations
- **Saved Games:** Games that you choose to save for later play

### Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we DON'T do:

- ❌ We do NOT collect any personal information
- ❌ We do NOT track your location
- ❌ We do NOT share your data with third parties
- ❌ We do NOT use analytics or tracking services
- ❌ We do NOT require an account or login
- ❌ We do NOT collect any data from your device beyond what's necessary for the game to function

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using:
- **AsyncStorage** (React Native's local storage system)

Your game data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any servers.

## Data Deletion

You can delete all your game data at any time by:
1. Uninstalling the app from your device
2. Using your device's app settings to clear app data
3. Using the "Clear Data" option in the app's settings (if available)

## Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

### Android
- **VIBRATE:** Used to provide haptic feedback during gameplay (optional)

### iOS
- **Haptic Feedback:** Used to provide tactile feedback during gameplay (optional)

All permissions are optional and the app will function without them.

## Children's Privacy

TAK Game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
- Including a notice in the app update notes

## Third-Party Services

TAK Game does not use any third-party services, analytics, or advertising networks.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your data:
- The right to access your data (all data is stored locally on your device)
- The right to delete your data (uninstall the app or clear app data)
- The right to know what data we collect (outlined in this policy)

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- Your data is protected by your device's security features
- We recommend using device encryption and secure lock screens
- Regularly backing up your device will preserve your saved games

## International Users

TAK Game can be used worldwide. All data processing occurs locally on your device, regardless of your location.

## Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for processing is:
- **Legitimate Interest:** To provide game functionality
- **Consent:** For optional features like haptic feedback

## California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

For California residents:
- We do not sell your personal information
- We do not collect personal information
- All data is stored locally on your device
